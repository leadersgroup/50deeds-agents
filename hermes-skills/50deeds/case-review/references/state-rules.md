# State rules for case review

Rules from past 50deeds reviews and template work, plus a few general
requirements. Not a complete
statement of any state's law: when a state isn't here, or a situation isn't
covered, check the team's county recording notes and say what you could not
verify.

## Minnesota
- Minn. Stat. § 507.02: a married owner's homestead can't be conveyed
  without both spouses' signatures (exceptions: purchase-money mortgage,
  conveyance between spouses, joint-tenancy severance; a spouse may sign by
  attorney-in-fact). A transfer to a trust is not an exception. When the
  deed says the owner is married and the county shows homestead, add the
  spouse as a grantor, update the acknowledgment, cover page and Section III
  signature block, and get the spouse's exact legal name from the client,
  not from a trust document.
- Well disclosure: the deed needs a truthful box checked. If all boxes are
  blank and the signing instructions don't tell the signer to check one,
  flag it before release. A prior deed's answer is a reason to ask, not
  proof of current status.
- Deed tax is a flat $1.65 when there is no consideration or it is $3,000 or
  less (§ 287.21). An irrevocable grantee trust doesn't change that when the
  deed consistently states consideration under the threshold.
- If the trust certificate says the trust is irrevocable, the owner is not
  the trustee, or the transfer looks like a permanent gift rather than
  ordinary revocable-trust funding, require written client confirmation and
  attorney review before release. It is not a recording defect.
- Abstract vs Torrens: leave the Torrens box unchecked when the county
  record type is abstract.

## Massachusetts
- Trustee's certificate under M.G.L. c. 184 § 35 needed for a trust grantee
  (current, recordable).
- Declaration of Homestead for Homes Owned by Trustee(s): blank beneficiary
  name (item 2) is fine. Do not flag.
- Recite grantor's marital status; a blank marital status field is an issue.
- Name linkage: the grantor's name on the deed must link to the name on
  record title and to the trustee name in the trust (add "also known as"
  where they differ).

## New Hampshire
- House rule for revocable-trust transfers: do NOT claim the RSA 78-B:2 XXII
  exemption. Pay the $40 minimum real estate transfer tax and use the agreed
  conveyance language. A deed or form still claiming XXII is an issue.

## New Jersey
- N.J.S.A. 46:26A-3: recording requires the preparer's name, lot and block,
  the grantee's mailing address, an acknowledgment, and names printed
  beneath every signature.
- GIT/REP seller's residency form (N.J.S.A. 54A:8-8 to 8-10) and an RTF-1
  Affidavit of Consideration (filed in duplicate) are required. The realty
  transfer fee exemption (46:15-10(a) under $100; (j) spouses/parent-child)
  must be claimed on the RTF-1.
- N.J.S.A. 3B:28-3: spouse's right of joint possession in the principal
  marital residence survives a deed the spouse did not join. Recite marital
  status; the spouse should join.
- Deed type (bargain and sale with covenant against grantor's acts /
  quitclaim / warranty) must be named.
- Five municipal forms: "Municipality of" is safer than a hardcoded
  "Township of".
- Mansion tax (from 7/10/2025) is paid by the seller and graduated
  (1% over $1M up to 3.5% over $3.5M).

## Rhode Island
- Attorney-prepared-deed state (In re Paplauskas, 228 A.3d 42 (R.I. 2020)).
- Recording is municipal (city/town Land Evidence Records, 39
  municipalities), not county.
- Quitclaim covenants (§ 34-11-18) are limited warranty. Statutory form
  § 34-11-12 Form 2. No witnesses; acknowledgment with stamp.
- Conveyance tax (§ 44-25-1) applies only above $100 consideration; there is
  no trust exemption, nominal consideration is why none is due.
- Affidavit of survivorship: § 34-3-1 presumes tenancy in common, so confirm
  survivorship words in the vesting deed first. The § 44-23-12 estate tax
  lien needs a Discharge of Estate Tax Lien (Form T-77).
- Record fast: the § 33-25-2(b) spousal protection depends on recording
  before the grantor's death.

## Oregon
- ORS 93.865 statutory form; ORS 93.030 true-consideration recital; ORS
  93.260 tax-statement address sentence (to the grantee/trustee, not the
  grantor); ORS 205.232 type-size floor; Oregon acknowledgment, not a
  California-style block.

## Texas
- Texas Property Code § 114.086 certification of trust for a trust grantee.
- § 11.008 confidentiality notice on the deed, at the top of the first page.
- No real estate transfer tax (Tex. Const. art. VIII, § 29) and no state
  value form. Gift deeds: recite no consideration, and exclude the implied
  covenants of § 5.023 if the deed is without warranty.
- Separate-property tracing recital where the property is one spouse's
  separate property.

## Pennsylvania
- REV-183 Realty Transfer Tax Statement of Value: all pages present and
  legible, the exemption checkbox selected. A "BEING" clause tying back to
  the vesting deed is expected.

## South Carolina
- Venue block and two witnesses; check the deed-recording fee affidavit
  exemption citation for a trust transfer.
- Affidavit exemption #8 (transfer to a trust): confirm from the trust
  evidence that the grantor is or becomes a beneficiary and that no
  consideration is paid other than the beneficial interest. If the trust
  evidence isn't in the file, raise it as a tax question; don't treat the
  exemption as verified.

## North Carolina
- Practitioners often expect a general warranty deed for trust funding.
- Consideration clause must match the $0 excise tax claimed.
- Warranty clause must not reference exceptions that are never stated.

## Arizona
- A.R.S. § 33-404: trust grantees must disclose beneficiaries (or the
  trust's beneficiary disclosure must be recorded). Grantor address required.

## Alabama
- Recordation tax (Ala. Code § 40-22-1): confirm the consideration and
  exemption treatment on the deed and the Real Estate Sales Validation form.

## Maryland
- Tenancy by the entirety: on a spouse's death the survivor owns by
  survivorship; no probate needed for that interest.

## New York
- SSN required for both grantor and grantee on every NY order.
- NYC transfer forms: check the smoke-detector affidavit is the right form,
  the resident certification is true for the grantors, and SSN/EIN fields
  are not swapped.

## Virginia
- § 58.1-811(A)(11) is the correct revocable inter vivos trust exemption
  cite. Do not flag.

## Vermont
- Death of a co-trustee: new deed names the survivor as sole trustee; needs
  the death certificate and a Certification of Trust.

## Florida
- Two witnesses; Florida acknowledgment states physical presence or online
  notarization.

## California
- Acknowledgment must be the California all-purpose form (Civ. Code
  § 1189) when notarized in California.

## Nevada
- NRS 247.110 first-page layout: 1" margins, a clear 3"x3" box in the
  upper-right corner, 10-pt minimum type. Measure with
  `scripts/measure_layout.py`.
- NRS 111.312: name and address for tax statements (the grantee) and the
  APN on the deed.
- NRS 239B.030 affirmation that the document contains no SSN; recorders can
  require it.
- RPTT exemption NRS 375.090(7) (to or from a trust) requires "without
  consideration" and a certificate of trust at recording; a "$10 and other
  valuable consideration" recital conflicts with it. Exemption (9) (to an
  entity the grantor owns 100%) fails when the individuals, not the trust,
  own the LLC. The Declaration of Value must match the deed.
- Recording goes to the county where the land is (Carson City mailing
  addresses are often in Douglas County).

## Mississippi
- Miss. Code § 89-5-24: 3" top margin on page 1 (3/4" elsewhere); name,
  mailing address and phone of the preparer and every grantor and grantee
  on page 1. Nonconforming documents record only with an extra fee.
- § 89-5-33(3): indexing instruction must give section, township, range and
  at least one quarter section; the clerk must refuse an instrument without
  it.
- No deed transfer tax. Unrecorded deeds are good between the parties but
  not against later purchasers or creditors without notice.
- Hunting-camp lots: a lot carved out of a club's master tract has no
  parcel ID until a deed is recorded and the assessor splits it.

## West Virginia
- Excise tax exemption for transfers without consideration between parent
  and child (and spouses, grandparent/grandchild, siblings): W. Va. Code
  § 11-22-1(10). Quitclaim and no-consideration deeds outside those
  relationships need the grantee's signature (§ 39-1-2).
- The State Tax Commissioner Sales Listing Form (STC 12:39) must be
  completed and must describe the same estate as the deed (e.g. a remainder
  subject to a reserved life estate, not "transfer on death").
